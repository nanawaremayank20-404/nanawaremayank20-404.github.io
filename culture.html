Festivals of Solapur

Siddheshwar Yatra

One of the largest festivals celebrated in honor of Shri Siddheshwar.

Ashadhi Wari

A famous pilgrimage where devotees walk to Pandharpur singing devotional songs.

Local Traditions

The culture of Solapur reflects a mix of Marathi and Kannada traditions. Music, devotional songs, and temple rituals are an important part of life.
