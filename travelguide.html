Travel Guide

By Road

Solapur is connected by highways to Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

  • Pune – 250 km
  • Hyderabad – 300 km
  • Mumbai – 400 km
  • Bengaluru – 380 km

By Rail

Solapur Railway Station is a major railway junction connecting Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and Bengaluru.

By Air

Nearest airports are Pune International Airport and Hyderabad International Airport.

Local Transport

  • Auto Rickshaws
  • Taxis
  • City Buses
